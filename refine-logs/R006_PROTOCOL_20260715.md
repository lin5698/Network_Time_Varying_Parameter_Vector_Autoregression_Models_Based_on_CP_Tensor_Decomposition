# R006 Approximate-Rank Pilot Protocol

**Freeze date:** 2026-07-15  
**Role:** M1 stop-go ablation; retrospective recovery pilot, not online prediction or rank-selection evidence  
**Compute route:** Apple M5 CPU through NumPy/SciPy linked to Apple Accelerate

## Fixed Grid

- `N=20`, `T=200`, trailing window 80, innovation standard deviation 0.25.
- Separation strength 0.15.
- Frozen-base query spectral radius in `{0.80, 0.95}`.
- Actual rank-3 approximation ratio `a3 / ||Theta*||F` in `{0, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50}`.
- Seeds `240100` through `240109`.
- Response horizon 8, stability threshold 0.98, projection sensitivity target 0.95.
- Final 30% of reconstructed dates form the evaluation band; the preceding 20% form the fused-TV validation band.

The truth contains eight mutually orthogonal CP components. The first three form the head. A common tail multiplier is calibrated by bisection so that the Frobenius norm of components four through eight divided by the full separated-block tensor norm equals the declared `a3` target after date-specific query-radius scaling. The observed panel is generated using the evolving topology, while recovery is evaluated at the fixed base topology.

## Methods

| Method | Role |
| --- | --- |
| local | unrestricted common local block estimator |
| spline-df10 | fixed temporal-only regression-spline projection |
| fused-TV | temporal-only 1D fused denoising; penalty selected from a frozen four-value grid on the validation band |
| CP ranks 2, 3, 5 | rank 3 is primary; ranks 2/5 diagnose under/over-rank behavior |
| Tucker ranks 2, 3, 5 | rank 3 is primary; ranks 2/5 diagnose under/over-rank behavior |

The fused-TV penalty multipliers are `{0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 1.00}` times each coefficient series' median absolute first difference. All candidates use the same local tensor. The selected penalty minimizes observed-topology one-step RMSE on the validation band. This is a retrospective tuning diagnostic, not a causal forecast protocol.

## Required Stored Metrics

- actual `a3` and `a5` approximation ratios;
- separated-block relative error;
- frozen-query relative operator error;
- raw finite-horizon response error;
- raw response error divided by the error of a zero-operator estimate;
- stability-qualified response error and qualification rate;
- projected sensitivity stored separately;
- paired improvement and paired win rate against local and fused-TV;
- CP multi-start final-objective spread;
- selected fused-TV penalty, runtime and numerical failure.

## Frozen Stop-Go Rules

1. Maximum absolute calibration error for `a3` is below `1e-6`.
2. For every cell in `a3 in {0.10, 0.25}` crossed with `rho in {0.80, 0.95}`, at least one prespecified rank-3 method, CP-3 or Tucker-333, satisfies all of:
   - paired median raw-response improvement of at least 10% versus local;
   - paired median raw-response improvement of at least 10% versus fused-TV;
   - response-error win against both baselines on at least 70% of seeds;
   - median relative frozen-query operator error below 1;
   - median response-error/zero-operator ratio below 1.
3. At least 95% of the expected method rows complete without failure.
4. Raw, stability-qualified and projected-sensitivity fields remain separate, and all truth query operators hit the declared radius within `1e-8`.
5. The `a3=0.50` cells are reported as a negative boundary whether they pass or fail. Rank-5 results cannot replace the rank-3 gate.

Failure narrows the claim to exact or very-near-low-rank paths. CP and Tucker remaining statistically indistinguishable narrows the method claim to block-preserving low-rank reconstruction rather than CP-specific superiority.
