# R006d Design-Weighted End-to-End Estimator Protocol

**Date:** 2026-07-15  
**Status:** `CONSTRUCTION_FAIL`; estimator implementation and outcome execution not authorized  
**Parent evidence:** corrected R006c `FAIL`, 2,160/2,160 rows, zero numerical failures, CP `0/16`, Tucker `6/16` with native `0/8`

## 1. Prespecified Question

Can one training-only, design-weighted end-to-end low-rank estimator improve recovery at `W_ref` and at two never-fitted supported topology endpoints in native endogenous designs, while rejecting a deliberately unsupported topology direction?

R006d is a method-screening gate. It does not presume that CP, Tucker or the new candidate remains the manuscript method. R007, N=50/100 expansion, native GNN baselines, EIA outcome inspection and manuscript promotion remain frozen until the complete R006d result-to-claim gate passes.

## 2. Why R006c Requires A Different Estimator

R006c rules out a narrow diagnosis in which only `B` estimation is defective. In required native cells, the oracle-B anchor uses true simulated `B` but retains median `W_ref` operator error of approximately 1.31--2.44 because its smoothed `M_ref` path is inaccurate. The next candidate must therefore change the estimation loss for the complete path; residualizing or smoothing `B` alone is ineligible as the sole new method.

The candidate must address two distinct problems:

1. **Native recovery:** use the realized design geometry when regularizing `M_ref` and `B`.
2. **Query support:** distinguish a topology endpoint that is algebraically evaluable from one whose slope direction is stably excited by the training design.

## 3. Claims And Anti-Claims

### Eligible claim if every gate passes

A fixed design-weighted end-to-end low-rank estimator improves reference and supported held-out topology response recovery in the frozen R006d simulation regime and abstains on a prespecified unsupported topology direction.

### Claims not licensed by R006d

- universal superiority across dynamic networks;
- causal effects of topology changes;
- topology formation or regime-switch identification;
- global convergence of the nonconvex optimizer;
- calibrated empirical uncertainty;
- scale beyond `N=20`;
- superiority to native temporal GNNs;
- performance on abrupt or non-factor temporal paths;
- cross-domain empirical generality.

## 5. Topology Endpoints

### 5.1 Reference endpoint

`W_ref` is the frozen base topology used by R006c. Its topology-slope difference is zero and its support certificate is defined as zero.

### 5.2 In-family endpoint

`W_alt_interp` retains the R006c construction:

```text
row_normalize(0.75 W_ref + 0.25 W_holdout_ER)
```

`W_holdout_ER` is generated by an independent stream from the same gamma-weighted directed Erdos--Renyi family. It is excluded from fitting and tuning. The single generated endpoint must pass the Section 6 support gate on the calibration region; retrying draws until one passes is prohibited.

### 5.3 Cross-family endpoint

`W_alt_family` is selected without outcomes from a fixed pool of 64 row-normalized directed degree-corrected stochastic-block candidates with four equal-size communities. Source and target propensities are independent `Gamma(shape=2, scale=0.5)` draws clipped to `[0.25,2.5]`. Before degree correction and the probability cap at `0.95`, within/between edge probabilities are `0.48/0.12`; positive edge weights are independent `Gamma(shape=2, scale=0.5)`. Diagonal weights are zero. A zero row receives one unit edge to its cyclic successor before row normalization.

For each simulation cell and seed, select the lowest-index candidate among those satisfying the supported-query construction gate in Section 6 using only calibration-prefix predictor designs. If no candidate passes, construction fails before any method fitting or validation score is produced. The candidate pool, family parameters and tie-breaking rule cannot change after validation or evaluation inspection. Report all 64 certificates, not only the selected candidate.

### 5.4 Unsupported endpoint

`W_alt_out` is selected without response targets from a separate fixed pool of 64 row-normalized candidates. The first 32 use the same degree-corrected construction with within/between probabilities `0.64/0.08`. The next 32 are hub-dominated: two deterministic opposite-index hubs per candidate, edge probability `0.70` when either endpoint is a hub and `0.08` otherwise, with the same gamma weights, zero diagonal and empty-row fallback. Using calibration prefixes only, rank candidates lexicographically by median-date then maximum-date support certificate and choose the lowest index among ties, subject to the unsupported threshold in Section 6. If no candidate reaches the threshold, construction fails before method fitting. Report all 64 certificates.

`W_alt_out` is scored only for abstention correctness and descriptive oracle error. It is never eligible to satisfy a recovery gate.

## 6. Query-Support Certificate

For each rolling prefix, construct direct design `X`, topology-exposure design `Z`, and

```text
Z_tilde = (I - X X_dagger) Z.
```

Let `s_max` be the largest singular value of `Z_tilde`. The numerical-support rule freezes the maximum retained inverse amplification at `kappa_max=50`:

```text
tau = max(1e-10, s_max / 50)
```

and let `P_tau` project onto right singular vectors with singular value at least `tau`. For `Delta W = W_star-W_ref`, define

```text
chi_tau(W_star)
  = || Delta W' (I-P_tau) ||_F / max(||Delta W'||_F, 1e-12).
```

Endpoint construction aggregates certificates over the disjoint calibration prefixes only. Validation and evaluation prefixes are never used to select an endpoint. After selection, support status is recomputed prospectively at every validation and evaluation prefix; a required endpoint that later becomes indeterminate or unsupported is an availability failure, not silently replaced.

- A required supported endpoint must have calibration `max_t chi_tau <= 0.10` and must retain that status at every scored prefix.
- The deliberately unsupported endpoint must have calibration and evaluation `max_t chi_tau >= 0.35` and median-date `chi_tau >= 0.20`.
- Values between the two regions are `indeterminate` and cannot enter a required endpoint.
- `kappa_max`, support thresholds, candidate-pool size and aggregation cannot be tuned against validation scores, endpoint errors or truth.
- If every direction is retained or no pool candidate reaches the unsupported threshold, abstention is not testable in this DGP and construction fails. The protocol must not manufacture an unsupported label.

Construction tests must verify the equivalent rank/kernel statement on exact low-dimensional examples and the decomposition

```text
Delta W' b = Delta W' P_tau b + Delta W' (I-P_tau) b.
```

### Required comparators

1. `block_local`: corrected scale-adaptive local ridge in `[A,B]` parameterization.
2. `block_fused_tv`: corrected causal-validation fused-TV reconstruction.
3. `block_tucker333`: existing same-target unweighted Tucker reconstruction.
4. `anchor_split_tucker333`: corrected R006c split smoothing of `M_ref` and `B`.

### Mechanism controls

5. `anchor_orthogonal_local`: Frisch--Waugh--Lovell residualized local ridge, transformed back to the original `M_ref,B` parameterization.
6. `anchor_orthogonal_tucker333`: unweighted split Tucker applied after the same orthogonalized local step.
7. `collapsed_ref_tucker333`: reference-map-only diagnostic; alternative endpoints are unavailable.
8. `oracle_b_dw_mref`: true simulated `B` is held fixed while `M_ref` alone is estimated under the same normalized design-weighted outcome loss and temporal rank constraint; ineligible for tuning or promotion.

### Sole promotion candidate

No CP candidate is eligible in R006d. This avoids another decomposition search after R006c CP passed `0/16`. CP may remain a historical comparator but cannot replace the fixed candidate.

## 8. Candidate Objective And Optimization

For each coefficient date `t`, its rolling loss uses only observations `s=t-window,...,t-1`:

```text
L_t(Theta_t)
  = sum_s || y_(s+1)
              - M_ref,t y_s
              - B_t (W_s-W_ref) y_s ||_2^2.
```

Let `D` be the number of fitted coefficient dates, `n_t` the rolling-window size and `Q_t=[X_t,Z_t]`. Define

```text
g_bar = mean_t trace(Q_t' Q_t / n_t) / (2N).
```

The complete normalized objective is

```text
(1/D) sum_t L_t(Theta_t) / (N n_t)
  + lambda_T g_bar / (2 N^2 (D-1))
      sum_t ||Theta_t-Theta_(t-1)||_F^2
  + lambda_B g_bar / (N^2 D)
      sum_t ||B_t (I-P_tau,t)||_F^2,
```

subject to Tucker rank `(3,3,3)` for the `N x 2N x dates` coefficient tensor and bounded factor normalization.

The frozen hyperparameter grids are:

```text
lambda_T in {0, 0.01, 0.05, 0.20}
lambda_B in {0, 0.01, 0.05, 0.20}
```

`g_bar` is recomputed from the available training prefix in each validation fold; no scale from a later fold is reused. Selection minimizes chronological observed-topology prediction RMSE using strictly rolling-prefix reconstruction. The final evaluation tail is excluded from all selection scores.

Optimization starts from `anchor_orthogonal_tucker333` and two deterministic perturbation starts. Each iteration consists of a design-loss gradient step, Tucker projection, factor normalization and post-projection objective check. Backtracking tries initial multipliers `1.00`, `0.50`, `0.25` in that fixed order and continues geometrically by `0.5`; these are optimizer mechanics, not validation hyperparameters. Select the start with the lowest training objective, never the lowest endpoint error. Freeze 300 maximum iterations, relative objective tolerance `1e-6` for five consecutive iterations and projected-gradient stationarity tolerance `1e-4`. Store every start's initial/final objective, accepted steps, objective trace, stationarity proxy, convergence status, runtime and pairwise solution distance. A row is promotion-eligible only if at least one start converges, the selected objective is finite and every accepted iteration is non-increasing. Failure is stored, not deleted.

Before the outcome run, numerical tests must show that the direct outcome loss and its design-Gram quadratic representation agree within `1e-8` on fixed toy windows.

## 9. Leakage And Chronology Contract

- Formal alternative topologies never enter estimation inputs, preprocessing, factor initialization, rank selection, penalty selection, stopping or start selection.
- Endpoint candidate selection uses only calibration-prefix topology and lagged predictor designs through `chi_tau`; it never uses validation/evaluation prefixes, response targets, truth blocks or endpoint errors.
- For each validation position, the candidate is fit only on coefficient-date prefixes available through that position.
- A later local estimate or outcome must not change an earlier validation score.
- Evaluation-tail response targets cannot affect hyperparameter selection. Lagged states from the evaluation tail may enter only the trailing-window estimate for a later scored date; they cannot change endpoint identity or any earlier support status.
- Matched and native panels use the same chronology rules.

## 11. Required Recovery Gate

The sole candidate passes a required cell only if all ten screening seeds satisfy availability and no-failure requirements and the aggregated metrics satisfy every condition below at `W_ref`, `W_alt_interp` and `W_alt_family`:

1. Endpoint availability is 100% and both alternative endpoints pass the support construction gate.
2. Median relative operator error is below `1.0` at every required endpoint.
3. Median response-error/zero-error ratio is below `1.0` at every required endpoint.
4. Paired median raw-response improvement is at least 10% against each of the four required comparators, separately at every endpoint.
5. Joint win rate against all four required comparators is at least 80%, separately at every endpoint.
6. For each comparator, define paired improvement as `1 - loss_candidate/loss_comparator` with denominator floored at `1e-12`. The median across seeds of the per-seed worst-supported-endpoint improvement is at least 10%. This is compatible with, and does not replace, the 80% joint-win rule.
7. Median chronological observed-topology prediction RMSE is no more than 5% worse than the best required comparator.
8. `W_alt_out` is returned as `unsupported` for every seed, with zero false-support rows.
9. All rows are finite, all stability fields are stored and no numerical failure is suppressed.

One fixed method must pass all 16 required screening cells. Cellwise switching, dropping native cells or relaxing thresholds is prohibited.

## 12. Screening, Confirmation And Stop Rules

### Screening pass

All 16 required cells pass under the ten inherited paired seeds. Only then may the 30 new confirmation seeds be generated.

### Confirmation pass

Apply the same unchanged gates to the 30 committed new seeds, requiring all cells to pass. Screening and confirmation summaries remain separate; they are not pooled to rescue failure.

The confirmation inferential unit is the seed, shared across all 16 cells. For each required comparator `c` and seed `s`, define the primary worst-case log improvement

```text
d_(s,c) = min over required cells and supported endpoints
          log(max(loss_c,1e-12) / max(loss_candidate,1e-12)).
```

For the four comparator contrasts, report exact one-sided sign tests of `d_(s,c) > log(1.10)` and simultaneous lower confidence bounds for the median using Holm adjustment at familywise alpha `0.05`. Promotion requires all four adjusted tests to reject and all four adjusted lower bounds to exceed `log(1.10)`. Cellwise medians, win rates and intervals remain fully reported; no unadjusted cellwise p-value licenses the claim.

### Mixed or failed result

- Matched pass/native fail: stop estimator promotion; design coupling remains unresolved.
- Reference pass/held-out fail: the method protects `M_ref` but does not preserve useful topology switching.
- In-family pass/cross-family fail: retain an in-family diagnostic only; no cross-topology claim.
- Orthogonalized control matches candidate: attribute any gain to residualization, not end-to-end low-rank estimation.
- Oracle B fails at `W_ref`: `M_ref` recovery remains the bottleneck.
- Candidate prediction loss exceeds the tolerance: no method promotion even if query ratios pass.
- Support construction fails: revise the design before outcomes; do not silently replace endpoints.
- Any required-cell failure: retain R006d as `FAIL`, keep R007 and scale frozen, and route toward a representation/diagnostic or specialist paper.

The outcome-free construction gate was executed after the protocol and tests were frozen. Artifact:

```text

```

SHA-256: `f18d33fba99232667f4bb5ad9efb7b62646a9f6d7769b85156c10eedd7760722`.

Observed construction facts:

- 160/160 seed-cell records returned `CONSTRUCTION_FAIL` solely because no `W_alt_out` candidate met the unsupported thresholds.
- `W_alt_interp` and at least one `W_alt_family` candidate passed support construction in all 160 records and retained support prospectively.
- all 9,600 calibration-prefix residualized designs retained rank `20/20` under `kappa_max=50`;
- smallest-to-largest singular-value ratios ranged from `0.1973` to `0.3596`, corresponding to condition numbers `2.78--5.07`;
- the largest unsupported share across all 10,240 unsupported-pool candidates was `2.60e-15`.

The failure is structural, not a pool-search shortfall: under this relative-conditioning rule, the corrected R006c design excites the complete slope input space. The protocol therefore terminates before estimator implementation. Threshold relaxation, larger candidate pools or relabeling a supported endpoint as unsupported are prohibited.

This result also limits interpretation. Full retained rank and `chi_tau` near zero establish absence of a discarded query component under the frozen rule; they do not establish small statistical error because absolute singular values and noise scale still govern variance. Any successor protocol must separate supported-endpoint recovery from an independently constructed low-dimensional excitation experiment for abstention.
