# R006b Stability-Signal Deconfounding Protocol

**Frozen before outcome inspection:** 2026-07-15  
**Scope:** controlled follow-up to R006; R006 remains `FAIL`

## Question

Does the rho contrast remain interpretable after query-operator magnitude, matched-design scale and ridge scale are held fixed? The experiment separates coefficient recovery from finite-horizon response transfer. It does not establish a general stability boundary.

## Construction

For orthonormal columns `u_r`, define

```text
M_t(W_ref) = sum_r lambda_{r,t} u_r u_r'
B_t        = sum_r c_r lambda_{r,t} u_r u_r'
A_t        = M_t(W_ref) - B_t W_ref.
```

The eigenvalue path fixes `rho(M_t)` at the declared stability level and fixes `||M_t||_F = 1.20` at every date. The concatenated component `[A_{r,t}, B_{r,t}]` is rank one. Tail energy is solved before simulation so that the component-energy rank-3 approximation ratio equals the declared `a3` target.

## Evidence Layers

1. `matched`: exogenous predictors and the complete separated design are identical across paired rho cells. Outcomes change with the declared operator and common innovations.
2. `native`: the same operator drives a recursive VAR. State covariance, innovation-to-state ratio and design-Gram spectrum are reported as outcomes.

The matched layer is the primary deconfounding diagnostic. The native layer quantifies endogenous state-scale coupling and is not required to have matched covariance.

## Frozen Grid

- `N=20`, `T=200`, rolling window `80`
- true component rank `8`, target rank `3`
- `a3 in {0.00, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50}`
- `rho in {0.80, 0.95}`
- layers `{matched, native}`
- 10 paired seeds `240100` through `240109`
- topology separation strength `0.15`
- innovation standard deviation `0.25`
- response horizon `8`
- adaptive ridge multiplier `0.001`
- methods: local, spline-10, validation-selected fused-TV, CP-3 and Tucker-333
- fitted methods use the same local estimates and the same adaptive-ridge rule
- expected rows: `2 x 4 x 2 x 10 x 5 = 800`

Fused-TV penalties are `{0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 1.00}` and are selected on the chronological validation segment. CP uses two starts, 50 iterations and a `1e-6` convergence tolerance. No method may tune on evaluation dates.

## Stored Metrics

- raw query-operator relative error
- raw finite-horizon response error
- response error relative to the zero-operator estimate
- stability-qualified response error and qualification rate
- projected sensitivity, stored separately and never substituted for raw results
- one-step prediction RMSE
- block coefficient error
- adaptive ridge penalty and Gram scale
- matched/native predictor RMS and covariance trace
- innovation-to-state variance ratio for the native layer
- design-Gram minimum/maximum eigenvalues and condition number
- method runtime, selected fused penalty and CP multi-start diagnostics

## Construction Gates

- query radius maximum absolute error `<1e-10`
- query Frobenius mismatch across rho `<1e-6`
- separated identity maximum error `<1e-10`
- second singular value of every component block `<1e-10`
- adaptive-ridge scale invariance error `<1e-8`
- matched-design covariance mismatch `<1%`
- a3 calibration error `<1e-6`
- all observed-topology radii finite and `<1.05`

The full-grid construction audit was completed before outcome inspection: maximum radius error `2.998e-15`, Frobenius error `6.661e-16`, identity error `2.220e-16`, a3 error `1.401e-08`, matched covariance mismatch `0`, and maximum observed radius `0.95149`.

## Outcome Gates

Required cells are the matched layer at `a3 in {0.10, 0.25}` and both rho values. Exact-rank and `a3=0.50` cells are diagnostics.

For every required cell, Tucker-333 must satisfy all of:

- paired median raw-response improvement versus local `>=10%`
- paired median raw-response improvement versus fused-TV `>=10%`
- joint paired win rate versus both comparators `>=80%`
- median query-operator error `<1.0`
- median response-error/zero-error ratio `<1.0`

The stability deconfounding gate additionally requires, for Tucker-333 in each required a3 level:

- paired median query-operator-error ratio `rho=0.95 / rho=0.80` in `[0.80, 1.25]`
- paired median response-transfer ratio at `rho=0.95` at least `10%` larger than at `rho=0.80`, where response transfer is raw response error divided by absolute query-operator Frobenius error

Completion requires 800 unique rows, no numerical failures, finite native state/design diagnostics, and separate storage of raw, stability-qualified and projected metrics.

## Decision Rule

- `PASS`: every construction, completion, required-cell and stability-deconfounding gate passes.
- `FAIL`: any frozen gate fails; the failed cells and diagnostics remain in the archive.
- Smoke runs can report only `SMOKE_PASS` or `SMOKE_FAIL`; they cannot pass the full protocol.

No threshold may be changed after outcome inspection. A failed R006b rules out a stability-boundary claim but does not erase the partial method claim already supported by R006.
