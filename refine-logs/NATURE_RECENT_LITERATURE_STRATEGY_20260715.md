## 1. Executive Decision

The next high-impact attempt should not begin with more scale rows, a new application or a stronger abstract. R006c identifies a prior scientific bottleneck: the current endpoint-aware candidates fail in the native endogenous design. Corrected R006c contains 2,160 rows with zero numerical failures, but `anchor_split_cp3` passes `0/16` required cells and `anchor_split_tucker333` passes `6/16`, consisting of `6/8` matched cells and `0/8` native cells. In native cells, the median operator and response ratios at the held-out topology are generally above one. This is not a presentation defect and cannot be repaired by projected metrics, oracle rows, collapsed maps, cellwise method switching or a larger N.

Recent Nature-family papers indicate that a broad computational advance normally combines five elements:

1. an irreducible computational object or operation;
2. a method that succeeds on the native target, not only a mapped surrogate;
3. genuine generalization to unseen structures, parameters, dynamics or geometries;
4. fair baseline, ablation and efficiency evidence; and
5. a consequential scientific use case with a visible failure boundary.

The manuscript has a credible object-level idea -- query preservation for topology-substitution responses -- and a clean exact non-identifiability construction. It does not yet have a successful native estimator, a statistical recovery theorem, cross-topology-family generalization or a consequential application. Therefore the immediate objective is a new, prespecified estimator question coupled to a topology-excitation certificate. Only a pass at that gate should release temporal misspecification, scale, native graph-baseline and public-application work.

## 3. What The Literature Changes In The Manuscript Diagnosis

### 3.1 The current object is necessary but not sufficient

The manuscript's strongest conceptual component is that storing only a collapsed map at one topology does not in general identify direct-only or alternative-topology endpoints. This resembles the representation arguments in temporal-memory and geometry-dependent operator papers. However, DIMON does not stop at saying geometry must remain evaluable: it supplies a trainable representation, approximation theory, unseen-geometry evidence, realistic scale and a large practical speedup. The present manuscript currently establishes an availability criterion and a finite-horizon perturbation transfer result, but not statistical recovery or successful native endpoint estimation.

### 3.2 R006c changes the estimator story

The anchor parameterization

`M_t(W) = M_ref,t + B_t(W - W_ref)`

removes algebraic cancellation at `W_ref`, but it does not create information about `B_t` when `(W_t-W_ref)y_t` is weakly excited or confounded with `y_t`. R006c shows this sharply: Tucker is strong in the matched construction but fails every native required cell. More importantly, the oracle-B diagnostic also fails the native `W_ref` absolute operator gate: with true `B`, median anchor-Tucker `W_ref` operator error remains approximately 1.31--2.44 across required native cells. The bottleneck is therefore not only slope recovery. The two-stage local-estimate-then-smooth pipeline fails to recover `M_ref` under native design coupling. The next candidate must optimize the outcome/design loss jointly over the low-rank path and must carry a topology-excitation certificate.

### 3.3 One held-out endpoint is not broad generalization

Recent papers use unseen geometries, dynamics, parameter settings, network structures or target layers. The current moderate-distance `W_alt_main` is useful as a leakage-free endpoint, but a single same-family topology cannot support a general topology-switch claim. A high-impact version needs at least three declared endpoint classes: unseen in-family interpolation, unseen topology-family transfer and an out-of-excitation-support endpoint at which the method should abstain.

### 3.4 Efficiency and uncertainty must become primary evidence

The strongest recent computational papers report wall time, memory, trainable parameters, scaling and uncertainty or feasibility. The current manuscript treats N=50 as bounded stress and does not yet have coherent series-level interval coverage. These are not immediate next steps because the estimator gate is still closed, but they become mandatory if that gate passes.

### 3.5 The applications do not yet supply immediate consequence

RCEP is carefully bounded and its re-estimation interval crosses zero. NYC is intentionally near-null. This is scientifically honest but weak as a broad-impact signal. The power-outage paper illustrates the missing ingredient: topology switching changes a concrete operational decision and infeasible actions are visible. A new public application should test whether a supplied-topology readout changes an interpretable propagation conclusion, while retaining the non-causal boundary.

## 5. New Prespecified Estimator Question

The next question should be frozen before any new outcome is generated:

> Can a training-only, design-weighted end-to-end low-rank estimator of the reference operator and topology slope, selected by chronological topology-blocked validation, improve both `W_ref` and never-fitted `W_alt` response recovery in native endogenous designs, while declaring unsupported topology directions rather than extrapolating silently?

A provisional candidate family is a **design-weighted end-to-end joint estimator**. This is a design target, not an approved method name or manuscript claim.

For each rolling training window:

1. Construct `x_t = y_t` and `z_t = (W_t-W_ref)y_t` using training dates only.
2. Residualize `z_t` on `x_t` within the available prefix, store the complete local design Gram and derive a query-specific support projector from its singular spectrum.
3. Optimize the original outcome loss jointly over the complete low-rank paths of `M_ref,t` and `B_t`; do not first create unweighted local coefficient estimates and then smooth the two blocks separately.
4. Use the design Gram, or the equivalent quadratic local likelihood, to weight coefficient directions. Weak slope directions are shrunk or marked unsupported rather than receiving the same Frobenius weight as strongly observed directions.
5. Include orthogonalized local and orthogonalized two-stage Tucker controls so any gain can be attributed to end-to-end design weighting rather than residualization alone.
6. Select all penalties and ranks by chronological validation on observed training topologies, including topology-blocked folds formed only from the training prefix.
7. Exclude every formal `W_alt` from fitting, preprocessing, rank selection, penalty selection and early stopping.
8. Evaluate `B_t(W_alt-W_ref)` only when an ex-ante topology-excitation certificate passes its frozen threshold; otherwise return `unsupported`, not a numerical success.

This candidate is motivated by the R006c failure and by recent joint representation/dynamics and hard-structure-constrained methods. It must still be rejected if a simpler orthogonalized local, fused or Tucker estimator performs equally well. The paper should promote the operation and certificate, not a bespoke acronym.

## 6. Required Theory Package

### T1. Query-specific representation theorem

Complete the existing R001/R002 direction: a stored representation supports a query exactly when the representation kernel is contained in the query kernel, equivalently when the query lies in the row space generated by stored topology evaluations under the declared block class. State necessary and sufficient conditions for single-topology, multiple-topology and structured-inverse cases.

### T2. Topology-excitation identification condition

For the anchor design, formalize when the residualized exposure process identifies the slope action. The central quantity should be the minimum eigenvalue of the residualized network-exposure Gram matrix on the relevant topology-action subspace, not a generic condition number. This theorem must explain why matched-layer recovery does not imply native recovery.

### T3. Supported-query decomposition

Decompose a held-out topology direction into its projection onto the training-excited topology-action subspace and an orthogonal remainder. The response-query error bound should contain:

- an estimable in-support term;
- a topology-distance multiplier;
- an approximation/regularization term; and
- an unsupported remainder that cannot be removed without an additional structural assumption.

This creates a principled abstention rule and prevents endpoint availability from being confused with statistical support.

### T4. Statistical recovery or oracle inequality

Derive at least one finite-sample or high-probability result for the joint estimator under declared temporal low-rank approximation, residualized excitation, noise and stability assumptions. A deterministic perturbation bound alone is insufficient for another broad methods submission.

### T5. Finite-horizon transfer

Retain the current finite-horizon companion perturbation result as the final link from operator error to response error. It should not claim CP recovery, rank-selection consistency or global convergence.

## 7. Construction Result And Successor Gates

The independently checked R006d protocol was stopped before estimator implementation. Its outcome-free construction gate returned `CONSTRUCTION_FAIL` in all 160 seed-cell records because no unsupported endpoint existed under the frozen rule. Every one of 9,600 calibration designs retained rank `20/20`; condition numbers were only `2.78--5.07`, and the largest unsupported share over the complete candidate pool was `2.60e-15`. Both supported held-out endpoint classes were feasible in all records.

This result changes the route in two ways. First, `chi_tau=0` is not evidence of accurate recovery: absolute singular scales still ranged widely, so query-specific inverse amplification must be reported. Second, abstention cannot be a required endpoint in the same full-rank DGP without manufacturing a failure case.

### Track A: supported-endpoint recovery

- retain `N=20`, `T=200`, the matched/native grid, ten screening seeds and 30 committed confirmation seeds;
- evaluate `W_ref`, `W_alt_interp` and construction-validated `W_alt_family` only;
- compare one fixed design-weighted joint Tucker estimator with local, fused, Tucker, anchor and orthogonalized controls;
- keep the existing absolute recovery, paired improvement, joint-win, worst-endpoint, prediction-loss, numerical and stability gates;
- report `chi_tau`, absolute singular spectra and the new retained-query amplification `alpha_tau` as diagnostics;
- do not claim abstention or use a support threshold to rescue poor recovery.

### Track B: exact-support stress

- construct topology perturbations in a prespecified rank-`r<N` exposure subspace;
- freeze one query inside that subspace and one with a nonzero orthogonal component;
- test kernel/projector equivalence, certificate classification and the supported/unsupported error decomposition;
- keep this track ineligible to promote the estimator or rescue a Track A failure.

Both replacement protocols require independent audit before implementation. A Track A pass can release R007 only if Track B also supports the theoretical classification claim; a failure in either track cannot be repaired by changing thresholds or endpoint pools.

## 10. Submission Decision Rule

Another Nature Computational Science or Nature Communications attempt is rational only if all of the following become true:

- a nontrivial estimator succeeds in native endogenous designs;
- theory explains both success and abstention;
- two unseen supported topology families pass;
- strong native and classical baselines are fairly evaluated;
- scale, cost and uncertainty are credible; and
- a public application yields a stable, interpretable consequence beyond a portability demonstration.

Nature Computational Science would require the reusable computational method and accuracy-efficiency story to dominate. Nature Communications would require a broader scientific finding or mechanism, not only an improved operator protocol. If the new estimator fails or the application is another near-null demonstration, the rational route is a strong specialist venue rather than further rhetorical broadening.
