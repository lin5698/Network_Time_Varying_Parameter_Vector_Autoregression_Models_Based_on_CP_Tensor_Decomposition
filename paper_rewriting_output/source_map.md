Purpose: establish which local materials may control scientific claims, which may teach writing strategy only, and which must remain outside the reader-facing manuscript. This map implements the launch-event principle without weakening the evidence boundary.

## Authority Order

1. `PAPER_CLAIM_AUDIT.md` and `EMPIRICAL_IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT.md` control empirical release status.
2. `manuscript_src/natcs/claim_evidence_ledger.csv` controls claim wording and prohibited extensions.
3. Exact proof packets, their independent receipts and immutable result artifacts control the facts they explicitly establish.
4. Current manuscript source files are drafts, not independent evidence.
5. Literature memos teach positioning and article architecture only. They cannot create a result or a priority claim.
6. Historical submission-readiness, portal and package records are superseded wherever they conflict with items 1-3.

## Launch-Event Routing Rule

Front stage: the scientific question, the query-certified computational object, exact capability certificates, two non-equivalent operator families once released, and evidence-supported operating regimes.

Back stage: authorization controls, hash binding, audit statuses, failed development routes and repository mechanics. These remain available to protect integrity but do not organize title, abstract, Introduction, Results headings or figures.

Claim-changing adverse evidence is never hidden. It is handled by narrowing the claim, qualifying the operating regime or withholding the claim until evidence exists. This serves **novelty / significance / rigour / clarity / generality / reproducibility / visual communication**.
