# Controlling Motivation Options After Research

Date: 2026-07-26

No option below authorizes a new manuscript claim. The selected motivation controls the future title, abstract, Introduction, Results order, figure sequence and evidence plan. Scientific wording remains bounded by the released claim ledger.

## Option A: Query-Certified Operator Learning

**Release sentence**

> We introduce query-certified operator learning, a computational framework that determines whether a learned evolving-network representation retains the arguments required for a declared scientific operator query and returns either a qualified response or a principled abstention.

**What the paper launches**

A reusable computational framework, not a CP model: query evaluability certificates, family-specific factorization conditions, endpoint-preserving estimation, stability/uncertainty qualification and abstention.

**Front-stage evidence**

1. Exact certificate for two non-equivalent operator families.
2. Same-endpoint recovery against fair native comparators.
3. Held-out topology, scale and horizon operating region.
4. Full-refit uncertainty and stability qualification.
5. One consequential descriptive use.

**Back-stage material**

CP implementation details, authorization records, SHA manifests, failed development routes, R006 labels and noncentral benchmark failures.

**Advantages**

- Directly answers the NCS desk-rejection concern with a broad conceptual/methodological contribution.
- Creates a distinct competition axis: certified scientific computability rather than generic prediction accuracy.
- Naturally accommodates the one-hop and second-order graph-filter families.
- Makes abstention a useful method output when supported by evidence.

**Risks and required repair**

- Current evidence is not sufficient for the full framework claim: C2/C3 remain unsupported and the bootstrap is not a full dynamic refit.
- The term `operator learning` may invite comparisons with neural operators; the Introduction must define the phrase operationally and distinguish it from architecture-specific neural-operator learning.
- CP should remain an implementation or be replaced by a stronger estimator only through an explicit research pivot.

**NCS fit**: highest upside; highest evidence burden. **Recommended.**

**Serves:** novelty / significance / rigour / generality / reproducibility.

## Option B: Query Preservation As A Representation And Identifiability Principle

**Release sentence**

> We establish a representation principle for evolving-network models: a scientific response can be recovered from a learned object only when the queried operator factors through the retained representation.

**What the paper launches**

A theory-led principle connecting retained representation, operator-family-specific identifiability and endpoint availability.

**Front-stage evidence**

1. One-hop unrestricted and structured diagonal results.
2. Second-order row-space/kernel theorem with exact positive and negative constructions once activated.
3. A compact numerical illustration showing the consequence of respecting or violating the certificate.

**Advantages**

- Best aligned with the strongest current evidence.
- Converts the paper from an estimator contest into a precise computational principle.
- Reduces dependence on universal recovery or application leadership.

**Risks and required repair**

- A senior editor may see it as an elegant design caution rather than a substantial method unless the consequence is shown across families and in a meaningful computation.
- The theorem must be stated broadly enough to be reusable but narrowly enough to remain proved.
- A positive capability, not only impossibility, must dominate Figure 1 and the abstract.

**NCS fit**: credible theory-first route; moderate editorial upside; lower evidence burden than A.

**Serves:** novelty / rigour / clarity / generality.

## Option C: Topology-Indexed Response Operators As A Reusable Computational Object

**Release sentence**

> We formulate topology-indexed response operators for evolving weighted networks, retaining the topology as a callable argument so that one fitted path supports matched structural response queries.

**What the paper launches**

A reusable response object and interface for observed, direct-only and alternative-topology readouts on one fixed coefficient path.

**Front-stage evidence**

1. Operator definition and exact endpoint classification.
2. Endpoint-preserving reconstruction in a controlled regime.
3. Stable response decompositions and one released application.

**Advantages**

- Closest to the current manuscript and therefore fastest to implement.
- Easy to explain visually and accessible to temporal-network readers.
- Keeps CP subordinate to the response object.

**Risks and required repair**

- Highest risk of repeating the desk-rejection outcome: an editor may interpret the operator as standard network/GVAR scenario analysis with new terminology.
- Requires a strong distinction between a callable operator object and ordinary topology-weight substitution.
- Without released empirical value, the contribution may remain specialist-facing.

**NCS fit**: clearest current-paper route; lowest disruption; highest “incremental object” risk.

**Serves:** clarity / rigour / visual communication / significance.

## Recommendation

Select **Option A** as the controlling NCS motivation, with Option B as its theorem spine and Option C as its concrete evolving-network instantiation. This is not a mixture of three stories: the hierarchy is `framework (A) -> certificate principle (B) -> network operator instance (C)`.

The first manuscript revision after confirmation should be structural rather than numerical: new title family, abstract, Introduction ending, Results headings and Figure 1-4 contracts. Numerical claims remain unchanged or absent until their evidence is independently releaseable.
