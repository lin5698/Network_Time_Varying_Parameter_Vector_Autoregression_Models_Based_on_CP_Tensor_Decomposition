# Confirmed Motivation

Date confirmed: 2026-07-26

## Author-Confirmed Direction

The manuscript will use **query-certified operator learning** as its controlling NCS framework, **query preservation and family-specific factorization** as its theoretical core, and **topology-indexed response operators for evolving weighted networks** as its primary instantiation.

This is one hierarchy rather than three parallel contributions:

> framework -> certificate principle -> evolving-network operator instance

## Red Thread

This paper addresses the risk that a learned evolving-network representation can fit observed dynamics yet be unable to answer a later scientific operator query. It introduces a query-certificate framework that tests whether the requested topology-indexed operator factors through the retained representation, instantiates the framework for separable response operators and evaluates an endpoint-preserving reconstruction in its supported regime. This matters because scientific response calculations should be returned only when their required arguments remain computable from the learned object.

## Reader-Facing Promise

The paper should enable a reader to ask of any compatible reconstruction:

1. What operator query will be asked after fitting?
2. Does the retained representation contain enough information to evaluate it?
3. If available, is the endpoint identified, recoverable and stable under the declared design?
4. If not, should the method abstain rather than return an uninterpretable number?

## Current Evidence Boundary

- Manuscript-usable: one-hop factorization boundary, diagonal structured inverse, controlled `N=15`/`N=30` recovery comparison and the frozen native-recovery qualification.
- Strategy-ready but not manuscript-activated: second-order Family-2 theorem, fixtures and blind proof review.
- Blocked: E3-v2 numerical outcomes, RCEP, NYC, broad transfer, calibrated uncertainty and consequential application claims.

## Launch-Event Rule

Title, Abstract, Introduction, Results headings, main figures and Conclusion will foreground the new capability and its strongest supported evidence. Internal governance, hashes, authorization history and failed development paths remain outside the reader-facing spine. Evidence that changes the truth of a central claim will narrow the claim or operating domain rather than be hidden.

## Reviewer Objectives

- **novelty:** make query certification, not CP, the contribution.
- **significance:** explain why post-fit scientific computability matters across computational models.
- **rigour:** tie each claim to a factorization condition or released experiment.
- **clarity:** maintain one framework-principle-instance hierarchy.
- **generality:** claim only operator classes and regimes actually established.
- **reproducibility:** describe scientific definitions and evaluation contracts without turning governance into the contribution.
- **visual communication:** organize figures as capability -> certificate -> recovery -> qualification -> use.
