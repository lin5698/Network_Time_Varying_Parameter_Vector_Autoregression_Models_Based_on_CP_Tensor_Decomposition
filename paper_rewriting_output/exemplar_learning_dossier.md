## Composite Article Blueprint Learned

1. **Release opening:** an important scientific computation can be invalidated by a representation that otherwise fits the observed system.
2. **Missing capability:** existing reconstruction objectives do not by themselves certify that a later operator query remains evaluable.
3. **New object:** a query-certified topology-indexed operator representation.
4. **Conceptual evidence:** family-specific factorization theorems and exact positive/negative constructions.
5. **Method evidence:** target-matched recovery in a predeclared operating regime, with abstention outside it.
6. **Generality evidence:** a genuinely non-equivalent operator family plus held-out topology, scale and horizon.
7. **Scientific consequence:** a prespecified descriptive result that changes only when the endpoint is certified and stable.

## Figure Grammar Learned

- Figure 1 should show the object and capability, not a governance flowchart.
- Figure 2 should show the two-family certificate geometry: retained row space, query row space, positive and negative examples.
- Figure 3 should show same-endpoint recovery and the operating region; method colour must remain constant across panels.
- Figure 4 should pair uncertainty with stability so a point estimate is never visually detached from its qualification.
- Figure 5 is justified only by a released scientific use whose conclusion matters independently of the method.

Use black/grey for shared structures, one warm accent for the proposed certified route, one cool accent for the same-endpoint comparator, and neutral hatching for abstention. Avoid a red-dominated “failure dashboard”; it makes the paper look like an audit report. **Serves: visual communication / clarity / rigour.**

## Language Grammar Learned

Preferred verbs: `preserves`, `certifies`, `evaluates`, `distinguishes`, `recovers`, `qualifies`, `transfers under`, `abstains`.

Avoid as headline language: `audits`, `fails`, `blocks`, `does not validate`, `pipeline`, `manifest`, `authorization`, `governance`. These words may be necessary in internal records, Methods or limitations, but they should not carry the reader-facing contribution.

## Next Most Valuable Modification

Freeze one release sentence that can order title, abstract, Introduction and Figure 1. Recommended: “We introduce query-certified operator learning, which determines whether a reconstructed evolving-network model retains the arguments required for a declared scientific response and returns either a qualified response or a principled abstention.” **Serves: novelty / clarity / significance.**
