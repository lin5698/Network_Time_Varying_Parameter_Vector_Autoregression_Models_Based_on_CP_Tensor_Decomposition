## Logic To Preserve

- Fixed fitted path, shock normalization and horizon define the query.
- Evaluability, identification, recovery and stability are distinct.
- Unrestricted one-hop factorization theorem and diagonal inverse exception must remain paired.
- Controlled recovery cannot imply native held-out recovery.
- Causal topology effects, formation and generic dynamics discovery remain outside scope.

## Logic To Replace

- `audit -> failure -> limited success` becomes `capability -> certificate -> supported recovery -> operating boundary`.
- `what cannot be claimed` no longer closes the abstract, Introduction or Discussion.
- CP/Tucker move from contribution labels to implementation/evidence roles.
