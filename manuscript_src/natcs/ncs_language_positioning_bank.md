# NCS Language And Positioning Bank

Purpose: provide evidence-bound wording that can be reused in abstracts, cover letters, portal fields, response letters and figure captions without drifting into overclaim. This is a working writing aid, not manuscript text.

Boundary: use only statements whose evidence appears in the current manuscript package. Do not use stronger variants until new data, raw-source permissions, DOI records or portal-preview evidence exist.

Serves: novelty / significance / rigour / clarity / generality / reproducibility / visual communication.

## Use Rules

- Keep CP as the implementation layer. Put query preservation and the topology-indexed operator in the headline position.
- Use "supplied topology argument" or "predetermined exposure matrix" for empirical readouts. Avoid language that implies topology formation, policy-effect identification or autonomous mechanisms.
- Attach benchmark gains to the replicated N=15/N=30 rows and to operator/GIRF recovery. Treat N=50, graph-feature rows and pair-level readouts as bounded stress or diagnostic evidence.
- Describe RCEP as a fixed-path topology-substitution readout with an estimation-path boundary.
- Describe NYC Taxi as a public second-domain same-operator check with a near-null aggregate topology contrast.
- Describe reproducibility as derived-evidence complete unless raw-source access decisions are confirmed.

## Short Portal-Field Variants

Use these when a journal field has tight word limits.

- One-sentence contribution: This Article defines query preservation for topology-substitution responses in evolving weighted networks through the operator `M_{k,t}(W)=A_{k,t}+B_{k,t}W`.
- One-sentence evidence: Replicated N=15/N=30 benchmarks show lower effective-operator and GIRF error for the endpoint-preserving CP implementation, while RCEP and NYC panels demonstrate bounded same-operator readouts.
- One-sentence boundary: The claim concerns conditional propagation under supplied topology matrices; topology formation, policy-effect identification and broad domain generality require separate designs.
- One-sentence reproducibility: The submission package regenerates manuscript evidence from derived objects, with raw-to-derived reconstruction documented under third-party access constraints.
