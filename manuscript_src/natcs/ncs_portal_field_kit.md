## Short Editorial Summary

Evolving weighted networks raise response questions in which the exposure topology changes while shocks, horizons and fitted coefficients stay fixed. This Article formulates that task as endpoint preservation for reconstructed dynamic-network response operators. The topology-switchable response operator keeps direct dynamics, network-mediated exposure and the supplied topology argument separately evaluable after smoothing. Controlled benchmarks test endpoint availability and recovery under declared comparison scopes. RCEP trade and NYC Taxi panels then illustrate fixed-path topology readouts with explicit uncertainty, stability and generality boundaries. The submission includes a reviewer code-and-derived-evidence archive that regenerates manuscript-facing evidence; raw-to-derived reconstruction remains governed by third-party source access and documented helper checkouts.

Tags: novelty / significance / rigour / reproducibility / clarity.

## Final Pre-Paste Check

Before pasting any field, verify:

1. The wording names query preservation or the topology-switchable operator before RCEP.
2. Benchmark gains are tied to replicated N=15/N=30 operator/GIRF recovery.
3. NYC is described as a public second-domain operator check.
4. Data/code wording says derived-evidence reproducibility, with raw-source constraints disclosed.
5. Fig. 2 wording does not claim portal-preview success until `ncs_fig2_portal_preview_checklist.md` is completed.
