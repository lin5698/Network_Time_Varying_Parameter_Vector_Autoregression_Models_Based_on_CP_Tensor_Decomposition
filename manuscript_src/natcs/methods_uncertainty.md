Recovery summaries are computed across synthetic replications. Figures report the median and interquartile range of each error metric; these intervals describe the replication distribution and are not confidence intervals or hypothesis tests. The primary $N=15$ and $N=30$ comparisons require matched 20-replication coverage for CP and unrestricted local rolling. At $N=50$, local, CP, Tucker, collapsed and low-rank no-network rows have four replications; sparse and projected graph-feature diagnostics have one each. These bounded stress rows are not pooled with the headline comparison.

Stability is recorded for every fitted path. Before a response error is computed, true and reconstructed effective operators with spectral norm at or above 0.95 are rescaled to 0.95 under the same rule. The original reconstructed operator is retained for qualification: the row-level instability rate is the fraction of target dates with spectral norm at or above 0.98. A finite stabilized response error does not override a high unscaled instability rate. Failed or non-finite reconstructions remain failures rather than being converted into favourable errors. Supplementary Table 2d reports instability and failure alongside recovery metrics.

The stricter endpoint-aware design is interpreted as a qualification gate, not as an additional average-performance table. Each fixed candidate is evaluated in 16 required cells, including eight native-design cells, with ten recorded seeds per cell. Promotion requires both recorded endpoints to pass in every required cell for the same candidate. The complete cell counts and promotion decision are reported in Supplementary Note 4; the main-text recovery claim remains confined to the original matched controlled design, while the representation-level endpoint classification is unchanged.

Proposition 2 provides a deterministic finite-horizon transfer statement conditional on bounded companion powers and a supplied coefficient error. The empirical stability screen and interquartile error summaries do not verify that assumption universally. They report whether the evaluated reconstruction remained numerically qualified under each declared benchmark design.
