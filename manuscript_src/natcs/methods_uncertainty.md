Uncertainty is reported in two layers. The window-wise moving-block residual perturbation layer re-estimates the local ridge and CP reconstruction stages under the selected data and tuning contract. Each rolling window independently draws residual blocks of 4 consecutive periods with replacement until the original residual length is reached. The pseudo-series keeps the first $p$ observed rows and recursively adds resampled residuals to one-step fitted values from the selected local model. Each draw holds the derived panel, time-indexed topology matrices, benchmark topology, lag order, rolling window, selected ridge penalty, selected CP rank, response definitions and downstream regression specifications fixed. It resamples residual blocks, re-estimates the local ridge stage, refits CP with the bootstrap settings and recomputes pair-level propagation contributions, aggregate propagation summaries, generalized impulse responses, observed-minus-frozen topology contrasts and RCEP association regressions. The manuscript analysis uses 500 bootstrap replications. Because windows are perturbed independently, this is a robustness sensitivity, not a single coherent pseudo-time-series bootstrap or a generic coverage claim.

The main CP fit uses 6 initializations, at most 100 iterations and tolerance 1e-6. Bootstrap re-estimation draws use 4 initializations, at most 80 iterations and tolerance 1e-5. Across bootstrap draws, local coefficients, CP factors and response summaries are re-estimated. The smaller bootstrap CP budget is a solver setting for repeated draws, not a hyperparameter-reselection rule. All reported 95% intervals are pointwise and conditional on the selected tuning contract. They do not provide simultaneous coverage across dates or horizons and do not include hyperparameter-reselection uncertainty. The reported uncertainty summaries use this 500-draw setting.

The second layer isolates the pair-level RCEP association on a fixed reconstructed path. The manuscript reports the baseline variance design together with unbounded, 1st-99th percentile trimmed and two-way clustered sensitivities. The coefficient is interpreted as a topology-sensitive generated-regressor association. The frozen/evolving comparison uses a conditional pair-cluster bootstrap on the reconstructed pair panel. Each draw resamples ordered country pairs, estimates the observed-topology and frozen-topology fixed-effects regressions jointly, and reports the observed-minus-frozen coefficient difference and frozen/observed ratio. These intervals condition on the reconstructed CP path. Near-zero denominators make ratio percentiles unstable; the manuscript treats frozen/evolving ratios as fixed-path diagnostics and uses the window-wise coefficient-difference sensitivity as the empirical boundary.

Stability is monitored from the spectral radius of the effective lag operator at each date. Dates with spectral radius greater than or equal to one are flagged explicitly. Supplementary Note 6 reports exclusion sensitivities and stability-projected sensitivities obtained by rescaling the date-specific lag blocks toward a target radius of 0.98. Supplementary Note 2 records the bootstrap settings, and Supplementary Note 6 collects the benchmark-dependence, clipping, stability and block-length sensitivity checks. Software versions, execution parameters and random-seed conventions are reported in Supplementary Note 7.
