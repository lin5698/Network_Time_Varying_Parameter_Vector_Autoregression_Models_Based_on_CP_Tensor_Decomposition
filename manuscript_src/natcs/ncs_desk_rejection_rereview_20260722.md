## Editorial conclusion

The revision improves the paper's first-screen logic. It replaces an estimator-first narrative with a query-first stopping rule, makes the endpoint gate visible in the two central figure captions, and preserves the negative endpoint-aware result as a boundary on claim inheritance. These changes materially improve novelty framing, rigour, clarity and visual communication.

They do not resolve the desk-rejection reason on their own. A broad computational-science claim still requires evidence that the audit changes outcomes beyond one separable operator family and one controlled benchmark. Until those data, comparator and reproducibility gaps are closed, the appropriate state is not a new NCS submission but a bounded specialist-journal reframing after venue-specific assessment.
