# Editorial Triage Brief

## One-sentence fit

Nature Computational Science readers care about representations whose downstream scientific computations remain executable, and this manuscript supplies query preservation as a checkable property of a topology-switchable finite-horizon operator, making representation-level query preservation a stated design target of the fitted object.

## Evidence that supports review

- Controlled one-hop benchmark at N=15 and N=30 with 20 replications per scale: median effective-operator error reductions of 93.6% and 96.8% and finite-horizon unit-shock response error reductions of 82.5% and 87.3%, each computed as the across-replication median reduction relative to unrestricted local rolling estimation.
- Exact kernel and row-space criterion for row-separable finite-basis operators, with constructive inverses under declared structure and explicit unsupported-readout identification before scoring.
- Endpoint-availability assignment precedes all numerical comparison; failure and instability records travel with recovery metrics.
- Frozen endpoint-aware qualification record (Supplementary Note 4) fixing the inheritance boundary between controlled gains and native held-out endpoints.

## Boundaries that should guide editorial interpretation

- No causal tariff-policy claim is made or licensed by any result in the manuscript.
- No exhaustive temporal-GNN forecasting benchmark claim is made; projected graph-feature rows are protocol diagnostics only.
- Controlled gains are simulation-truth statements inside the declared data-generating process; they are not independent empirical validation.
- CP is the implementation layer for every reported controlled comparison, while the contribution under review is the representation criterion itself.
- Manuscript-promotion authorization for the corrected empirical runs remains a separate author decision (RC-1); claim activation stays gated by open characterization flags (RCEP F1-F3, NYC characterizations).
- All empirical quantities cited here trace to the frozen controlled-benchmark contract and its evidence bundle.
