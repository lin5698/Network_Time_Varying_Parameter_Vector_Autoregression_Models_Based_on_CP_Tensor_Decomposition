Scientific models of evolving networks are often reused for computations beyond observed-data fit. Compression can remove required inputs even when predictive fit survives: two coefficient pairs sharing one retained map on the observed topology can define different operators once that topology is replaced. We introduce query-certified operator learning, which tests whether a requested computation factors through the retained representation, making query preservation a checkable property of the fitted object. For topology-indexed dynamics we derive an exact kernel criterion for row-separable finite-basis operators. It covers one- and two-hop topology bases, yields a structured diagonal inverse and marks unsupported readouts before scoring. In 20-replication controlled one-hop benchmarks at N = 15 and N = 30, endpoint-preserving CP reduced median effective-operator error by {{operator_gain_n15}}% and {{operator_gain_n30}}%, and median finite-horizon unit-shock response error decreased by {{response_gain_n15}}% and {{response_gain_n30}}% relative to unrestricted local rolling. Retained objects can declare the computations they preserve.
