Scientific models are often reused for computations beyond observed-data fit. Yet compression can remove the inputs required by those computations even when it preserves predictions under observed conditions. We introduce query-certified operator learning, which tests whether a requested scientific computation factors through the retained representation. For topology-indexed dynamics, we derive an exact kernel criterion for row-separable finite-basis operators. The criterion covers one- and two-hop topology bases, yields constructive inverses under declared structure and identifies unsupported readouts before numerical scoring. In controlled one-hop benchmarks, endpoint-preserving CP reduced median effective-operator error by {{operator_gain_n15}}% and {{operator_gain_n30}}% across 20 replications at N = 15 and N = 30. Median finite-horizon unit-shock response error decreased by {{response_gain_n15}}% and {{response_gain_n30}}% relative to unrestricted local rolling. Query certification makes downstream computability an explicit target of scientific representation design.
