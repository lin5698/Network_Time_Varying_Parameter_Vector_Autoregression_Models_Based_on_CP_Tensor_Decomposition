Scientific models of evolving networks are often reused for computations that reach beyond observed-data fit. Compression can remove required inputs even when predictive fit survives. We introduce query-certified operator learning, which tests whether a requested computation factors through the retained representation, making query preservation a checkable property of the fitted object. For topology-indexed dynamics we derive an exact kernel criterion for row-separable finite-basis operators. The criterion yields an exact unrestricted-block boundary and a structured diagonal inverse, covers one- and two-hop topology bases and identifies unsupported readouts before numerical scoring. In controlled one-hop benchmarks, endpoint-preserving CP reduced median effective-operator error by {{operator_gain_n15}}% and {{operator_gain_n30}}% across 20 replications at N = 15 and N = 30. Median finite-horizon unit-shock response error decreased by {{response_gain_n15}}% and {{response_gain_n30}}% relative to unrestricted local rolling. Query certification makes computability an explicit design target: retained objects ship with a statement of the computations they preserve.
