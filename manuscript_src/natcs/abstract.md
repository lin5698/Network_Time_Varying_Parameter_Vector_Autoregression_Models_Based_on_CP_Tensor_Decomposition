Topology-dependent response queries ask how a fixed shock propagates under an alternative exposure graph. Smoothing can erase the direct and network components needed to answer that query. We formulate topology substitution on a time-varying network autoregression and distinguish endpoint definition, identification, numerical recovery and finite-horizon stability. A separated representation defines observed, zero-network and benchmark-topology responses; a collapsed map needs an identified inverse. In the controlled N=15 and N=30 benchmark, separated CP reduced effective-operator error by {{baseline_coef_gain_replicated_range}}% and impulse-response error by {{baseline_girf_gain_replicated_range}}% relative to unrestricted rolling estimation. A stricter endpoint-aware benchmark did not validate broad native recovery for the tested CP or Tucker candidates. Trade and mobility panels establish bounded fixed-path computability: the trade contrast includes zero after path re-estimation, and the mobility contrast is near zero. Response-query preservation is a representation criterion; recovery and causal interpretation require excitation and appropriate design.
