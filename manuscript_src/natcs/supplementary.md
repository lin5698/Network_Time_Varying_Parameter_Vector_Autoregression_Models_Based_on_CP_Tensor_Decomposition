This Supplementary Information document gives the methodological, benchmark and reproducibility details that are shortened in the main manuscript. It contains eight supplementary notes covering notation, the estimator, propagation metrics, the larger-N benchmark grid, the empirical construction, robustness inventory, reproducibility materials, and the operating regime of the operator.

The supplement gives the estimator in enough detail for review while keeping the main text focused on the core computational claims. It documents the rolling-local plus CP-reconstruction estimator used in the synthetic and empirical sections, together with the operating conditions that govern interpretation.

The empirical notes document how the propagation series is constructed and qualified: temporally disaggregated quarterly trade inputs, generated-regressor inference in the second-stage association, topology-freeze conventions, variance sensitivities and stability diagnostics.
