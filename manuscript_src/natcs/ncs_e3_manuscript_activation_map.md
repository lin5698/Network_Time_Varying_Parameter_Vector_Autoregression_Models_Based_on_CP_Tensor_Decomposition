## NCS Re-entry Rule

The NCS route is method-led. Reconsider submission when the representation
criterion is supported as a broad computational abstraction, C2 has
claim-ready same-endpoint recovery and uncertainty/stability evidence, the
controlling audits are releaseable, and a fresh result-to-claim audit binds
every sentence and figure to evidence. C3 is an optional significance
amplifier, not a mandatory condition for a conceptual/methodological Article;
it becomes mandatory only if the manuscript claims practical consequence in a
public network. A new three-perspective NCS re-review must then judge whether
the editor's broad-interest concern has been addressed by the method and its
evidence rather than by framing alone.
