### RCEP descriptive topology substitution

The fixed-path RCEP readout compares supplied observed and frozen trade-exposure matrices under the same shocks, horizon and reconstructed coefficient path. It is a descriptive topology-sensitive generated-regressor association, not a tariff-policy causal estimate, an endogenous network-formation model or a mechanism-identification result.

At the pair level, the observed and frozen specifications give coefficients of {{rcep_baseline_pair_coefficient}} and {{rcep_frozen_pair_coefficient}}, respectively. The aggregate H=8 point-series mean difference is {{rcep_pre_point_difference}} before 2022 Q1 and {{rcep_post_point_difference}} afterward; bootstrap median differences average {{rcep_pre_bootstrap_difference}} and {{rcep_post_bootstrap_difference}}. These values are reported as descriptive readouts under the fixed-path estimand.

Across the declared topology-perturbation diagnostic, mean 0.0023 and median 0.0011 absolute changes are observed; this is a sensitivity summary and not a causal effect estimate.

The period split and aggregate comparison are subject to two explicit limitations: the GIRF date mapping is not documented, and the evolving point estimate lies outside its bootstrap percentile interval. The stable-dates-only pair-level regression remains **not identified** because `TC_relief` is collinear with pair and time effects; it is not interpreted as a positive or negative finding. No RCEP number is used to support a policy or causal claim.
