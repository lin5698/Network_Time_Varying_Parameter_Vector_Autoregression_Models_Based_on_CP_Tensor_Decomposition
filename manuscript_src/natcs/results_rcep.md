In the RCEP trade network, the switchable operator turns one reconstructed coefficient path into matched topology readouts. We use quarterly observations for the 15 RCEP economies from 2005 Q4 to 2024 Q4 and predetermined exposure matrices from lagged bilateral trade weights. The readout compares the observed topology with the 2016 Q1 to 2019 Q4 benchmark topology while holding shocks, horizons and coefficients fixed. A window-wise moving-block residual perturbation tests how this fixed-path contrast changes when the local and CP stages are re-estimated. The application is descriptive: the coefficient-difference sensitivity interval includes zero, while the reconstructed path remains inside the monitored unit-radius threshold on all 36 retained dates.

The coefficient readout is positive on the fixed reconstructed path. The pair-level association compares generated network contributions under alternative topology arguments and is secondary to the operator-level and aggregate readouts. The baseline regression has 7560 observations from 210 ordered non-self country pairs over 36 post-burn-in dates, with pair and quarter fixed effects and pair-clustered standard errors. In the baseline import-share specification, the observed-topology coefficient is 0.000873 with a standard error of 0.000355; the fixed-path frozen-topology coefficient is 0.000693. The observed-minus-frozen coefficient difference is 0.000180, with a 2000-draw pair-cluster interval of 0.000022-0.000530. The conditional frozen/observed ratio is 79.4%, with interval 74.3-87.5%. A 500-draw window-wise moving-block residual perturbation gives a coefficient-difference interval of -0.000071 to 0.000470, which bounds directional interpretation.

Pre-specified topology summaries provide descriptive topology-sensitive measurement context for the aggregate propagation series. Figure 3d and Supplementary Figure 6, with the complete metric inventory in Supplementary Table 8, show that the largest Spearman associations in the fitted readout involve spectral gap, directional asymmetry and lower spectral effective rank. These correlations are descriptive diagnostics for the fitted readout and do not identify a mechanism or causal effect.

Topology-construction and stability checks set the empirical boundary. Import-based, export-based, symmetric and longer-window topology definitions keep the pair-level point estimates positive, with different magnitudes and uncertainty levels. A controlled topology perturbation attenuates the highest import-exposure economy by 50% at each date before row-normalization; this topology-perturbation sensitivity check increases the aggregate propagation index by a mean 0.0023 and median 0.0011 (Supplementary Figure 7). The reconstructed CP path remains inside the unit-radius threshold on all 36 retained dates (Supplementary Figure 2). Stability-qualified and stability-projected summaries consequently coincide with the full reconstructed path in this rerun. These checks support fixed-path topology-sensitive summaries and bound directional aggregate interpretation.
