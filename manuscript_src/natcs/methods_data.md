The empirical workflow starts from a response panel and a predetermined sequence of exposure matrices. The RCEP panel combines quarterly macro indicators from IMF IFS and national statistical offices, annual multi-regional input-output information used to construct value-added trade proxies, official RCEP tariff schedules and bilateral import data used for network weights. It covers the 15 RCEP economies from 2005 Q4 to 2024 Q4.

Annual value-added trade proxies are converted to quarter-end series by a Chow-Lin-style proportional allocation. Within each country-year, the annual benchmark is distributed across quarters in proportion to the available quarterly indicator, using total exports by default. If no quarterly indicator is available, the annual value is divided evenly across four quarters. Gaps of at most two quarters are linearly interpolated, and larger gaps are left to the documented derived-data construction. The resulting series is aligned with macro indicators, tariff schedules and lagged bilateral trade weights.

Network matrices are predetermined weighted exposure matrices for the response recursion. The RCEP baseline uses lagged bilateral import shares over a four-quarter window, sets the diagonal to zero and row-normalizes each date-specific matrix. Export-based, symmetric and longer-window alternatives test whether operator responses depend on the topology-construction rule. Direct gross-trade alternatives and disaggregation stress tests are retained as robustness checks. The exposure-recursion assumption does not identify causal network formation.

Raw IMF, tariff, bilateral trade and licensed MRIO inputs are documented through acquisition notes. The public NYC Taxi panel uses the released Manhattan yellow-taxi tensor and rolling trip-share exposure matrices described in the Results and Supplementary Methods. Redistributable derived panels, time-indexed exposure matrices, benchmark summaries and bootstrap outputs are sufficient to reproduce the reported figures and tables. Reconstructing these panels from source files requires access governed by the original providers; the acquisition notes record sources, transformations and version information.
